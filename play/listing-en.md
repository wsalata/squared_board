# Title (17/30)

Times Tables Grid

# Short description (63/80)

Multiplication and division up to 100 on squared paper. No ads.

# Full description (2480/4000)

Times Tables Grid is a calm app for learning multiplication and division up to 100, made for children in their first school years.

It looks like a squared exercise book: paper, navy ink and hand-drawn buttons. No flashing banners, no rush, no surprises.

TWO MODES

• Play — a round of ten questions, ending with zero to three stars.
• Race the clock — sixty seconds, counting correct answers. The app remembers the best score.

FOUR KINDS OF FACTS

• Multiplication: 3 · 4 = ?
• Division: 12 : 4 = ?
• Mixed: one, then the other
• Puzzles: ? · 4 = 12 — find the missing part of the equation

STICKERS

The stars finally buy something. Mastering a whole table, passing a star threshold, setting a race record or playing several days in a row each earns a sticker for the album — sixteen drawings in the same hand as the rest of the app. The empty slots are labelled, so it is clear from the start what is still out there. A sticker once earned can never be lost.

A DAILY STREAK

The home screen shows the last seven days with ticks, plus a short challenge of the day: five questions drawn from whatever is going worst, once a day, for a bonus star. Any finished round keeps the streak alive, not just the challenge — a child who simply plays a lot loses nothing.

MY BOARD

A ten by ten board shows in colour which facts are already known. Every fact mastered turns into a green square, so progress is visible at a glance. Tapping a square shows the fact and how well the child knows it.

TEACHES SMARTER THAN RANDOM

Questions are not drawn evenly. A fact the child struggles with comes back several times more often than one already mastered. A question just asked does not reappear straight away. The wrong answers on offer are neighbouring multiples and off-by-one slips, not random numbers — the child has to work it out rather than guess.

FIT IT TO THE CHILD

• Choose which tables to practise, from one to ten
• Answer by picking one of four tiles, or by typing the result
• Sound and haptics can be turned off

PRIVACY

• No advertising
• No in-app purchases
• No accounts, no sign-in
• The app does not even hold the internet permission — nothing can be sent from it
• Progress stays on the phone

ACCESSIBILITY

Equations are read out by screen readers in spoken form. The app honours the system setting that turns animations off. On narrow screens the equation shrinks instead of being clipped.

The app is available in Polish and English. The source code is public.
