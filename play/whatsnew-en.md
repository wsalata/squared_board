# What's new (464/500)

New: stickers and a daily streak.

• Sixteen stickers to earn — for mastering a whole table, for passing star thresholds, for a race record and for playing several days in a row. Earned ones go into the album, and the empty slots say what would open them. A sticker once earned can never be lost.

• Challenge of the day — five questions drawn from whatever is going worst, once a day, for a bonus star.

• A row of seven days on the home screen shows your streak.
